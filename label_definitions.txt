# TakeMeter Label Definitions
# Community: r/technology
# Each line follows the format: label: definition

analysis: A comment that explains a technology-related issue using specific facts, examples, statistics, or clear supporting reasoning.
hot_take: A comment that expresses a strong opinion or judgment about a technology-related issue without providing substantial supporting evidence.
reaction: A comment that primarily expresses an immediate feeling, such as surprise, excitement, frustration, or concern, about technology-related news or events.
